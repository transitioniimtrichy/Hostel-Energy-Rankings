How we calculate this

  • Fair comparison. Hostels differ in size, so the winner is the hostel with the lowest energy use per resident.
  • Energy saved. How much less each resident used than last month, multiplied by this month's residents, so a hostel that fills up is not penalised.
  • Everyday comparisons (rough averages). 1 kWh of grid electricity causes about kg of CO₂ (India grid average, CEA). A mature tree absorbs about kg of CO₂ a year, a petrol car emits about kg per km, a phone charge uses about kWh, a 1.5-ton AC uses about kWh an hour, and generating 1 kWh burns roughly kg of coal.
