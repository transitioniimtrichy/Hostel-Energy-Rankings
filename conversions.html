How we turn kWh into everyday terms

A number like 5,050 kWh is hard to picture. These are the formulas we use to explain it as CO₂, trees, car travel, phone charges, AC hours and coal.

The formulas

Every comparison starts from the electricity a hostel used (kWh). The factors come from config.js, so this table always matches the ranking page.

How the ranking works

  • Per capita use = total kWh ÷ number of residents. The hostel with the lowest figure is the winner, because it is fair to hostels of different sizes.
  • Total use = total kWh the hostel used in the month.
  • Use per room = total kWh ÷ number of rooms.
  • Energy saved = (last month's kWh per resident − this month's kWh per resident) × this month's residents. It starts from the second month of data. A hostel that gains residents is not penalised.

A note on accuracy

The CO₂ factor is the national grid average, so it is a good estimate but not a measurement of our own campus supply. The other factors (trees, car, phone, AC, coal) are rough everyday averages, meant to make numbers relatable rather than to be exact. To change any factor, edit config.js.
